What We
Offer

Head Coach Konmon Deh brings over 20 years of combat sports experience to every service he offers. As a Double European Champion and the strategic mind behind three reigning world and European champions, his coaching is rooted in proven elite-level methodology — not just theory.

Whether you're looking for personalized one-on-one training, group classes, fight preparation, or fitness-focused sessions, every program is designed to push you beyond your limits while keeping you safe and technically sound.

From beginners taking their first steps in combat sports to professional fighters preparing for title fights, Konmon tailors his approach to meet your specific goals and skill level. He works with children, adults, and seniors alike.
