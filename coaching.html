FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I freeze my membership?+
Members can freeze their membership for up to 3 months per year with no additional charge. Contact us at least 5 days before your next billing date.
Is there a joining fee?+
The Unlimited plan has no setup fee. The Gym Only and Pay-As-You-Train plans have a one-time $15 card fee covering your access card and member profile setup.
What's included in a free class?+
Free classes cover any scheduled group session including MMA Fundamentals, Boxing Conditioning, Kickboxing, and Strength & Conditioning.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Monthly memberships can be cancelled after a minimum 6-month commitment. The Pay-As-You-Train annual plan is non-refundable after the first 14 days.
Do you offer corporate memberships?+
Yes! We offer tailored corporate wellness packages for teams of 5 or more. Contact us and a membership manager will reach out within 48 hours.
Can I bring a guest?+
Unlimited members receive unlimited guest passes. Other plans can purchase guest day passes at the front desk for $12 per visit.
